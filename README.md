# Sportspedia – HCI Design Project

Sportspedia is a Human–Computer Interaction (HCI) design project focused on improving **sports literacy and accessibility of sports-related information**. The project explores user needs, design thinking, and iterative prototyping to create an intuitive platform that helps users learn about sports, follow updates, and explore sports career pathways.

The repository contains the **complete design process**, including requirement gathering, surveys, personas, low-fidelity and high-fidelity prototypes, and evaluation results.

---

# Project Overview

Many people face barriers when exploring sports due to **limited awareness of sports rules, events, and professional opportunities**. Sportspedia aims to address these challenges by providing an accessible and engaging platform that educates users about sports and promotes greater participation.

The project follows a **user-centered design methodology**, starting from problem identification and requirement gathering to prototype development and usability evaluation.

The goal is to create a **structured and engaging ecosystem for sports knowledge, updates, and career guidance**.

According to the project research, sports literacy in India is estimated to be around **5.56%**, highlighting the need for platforms that promote awareness and participation.

---

# Problem Statement

People often face challenges when exploring or pursuing sports because they lack information about:

- Rules and structure of different sports
- Career pathways in sports
- Events and tournaments
- Athlete recognition and achievements

These barriers limit public participation and prevent many individuals from developing an interest in sports.

---

# Project Objectives

The main objectives of the Sportspedia platform are:

- Promote **sports literacy**
- Increase awareness about **different sports and their rules**
- Provide **structured learning resources**
- Deliver **real-time sports updates and news**
- Offer **career guidance in sports**
- Encourage community engagement among sports enthusiasts

---

# Key Features of the Proposed Platform

### Sports Education
- Rules and regulations of different sports
- Required equipment and gameplay strategies
- Beginner-friendly learning modules

### Real-Time Sports Updates
- Daily sports news
- Athlete performance highlights
- Tournament updates

### Athlete Recognition
- Dedicated sections highlighting top athletes
- Success stories and achievements

### Career Guidance
- Information on professional pathways in sports
- Training and development opportunities
- Mentorship and networking resources

### Community Engagement
- Discussion forums
- Competitions and leaderboards
- User interaction and collaboration

---

# Target Users

Sportspedia is designed for a wide range of users:

- **Sports Enthusiasts** – Users who want to deepen their knowledge of sports  
- **Aspiring Athletes** – Individuals looking for career guidance in sports  
- **Parents & Guardians** – People supporting children pursuing sports  
- **Educators & Coaches** – Trainers seeking educational sports resources  
- **General Public** – Anyone interested in learning about sports  
- **Corporate Sponsors** – Organizations interested in sports trends and growth  

These stakeholders were identified through interviews, surveys, and user research conducted during the project.

# Design Process

The project followed a structured **HCI design workflow**.

### 1. Problem Understanding
- Identifying issues in sports literacy
- Analyzing the current sports awareness gap
- Understanding user pain points

### 2. Requirement Gathering
- Surveys and interviews
- Stakeholder identification
- User mental models and mind maps

### 3. Persona Creation
- Developed multiple user personas to represent different user groups
- Built empathy maps and scenarios to understand user behavior

### 4. Low-Fidelity Prototyping
- Hand-drawn wireframes
- Interface structure and navigation design
- Iterative improvements based on feedback

### 5. High-Fidelity Prototyping
- Interactive Figma prototype
- Visual interface and usability enhancements
- Final UI layout and interaction design

### 6. User Testing & Evaluation
- Usability testing using survey forms
- User feedback collection
- Iterative improvements based on evaluation
